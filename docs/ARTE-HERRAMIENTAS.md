## Instrucción compartida de edición

Edit this illustration into a substantially cleaner, calmer and more elegant version for the MomentumVelo app. Preserve the main subject and wide landscape format, but radically reduce visual clutter. Premium modern technology aesthetic, precise thin luminous edges, restrained turquoise with one soft violet accent, natural glass reflections, moderate brightness, a smooth deep navy background with generous breathing room. The subject should be beautiful, clear and dimensional, not a flat icon. Remove all starbursts, sparkles, particle fields, light trails, energy ribbons and dense wireframe patterns. No blazing white highlights, no hot pink, no intense bloom. No lettering, numbers, charts, UI or logos. The image is decorative conceptual art.

## Indicaciones por sección

- Mercados: Keep just one polished translucent orbital sphere with a simple inner compass form and two fine orbital arcs. Remove the small surrounding nodes and stars. The sphere occupies the middle 60 percent of the frame.
- Radar: Keep one elegant tilted radar disc with only three concentric rings, a soft cyan sweep and three small signal dots. Remove the floating crystal diamonds, outer ribbons and background grid. Plenty of clear space.
- Cartera: Keep ONLY a sophisticated translucent closed briefcase and a slim open notebook beside it, sitting on a very subtle reflective surface. Remove the bull, globe, floating geometric tokens and all surrounding effects completely. Minimal, calm composition with turquoise edge light and a subtle violet reflection.
- Academia: Keep an elegant open glass book with just a few simple pages, with a small graduation cap floating quietly above it. Remove the light plumes, ribbons, stars and glowing page explosion. Soft turquoise edges and subtle violet glass, calm inviting learning imagery.
- Comparador: Keep two refined translucent faceted prisms, turquoise on the left and soft violet on the right, with a single very thin curved connector between them. Remove the central starburst, ground crystals, orbit loops, particle field and all energy effects. Calm balanced gallery-like composition.
